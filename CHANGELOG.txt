# Changelog

All notable changes are logged here (one file, newest first).

## 4.7.5 - 2026-10-08
- Trade form: new field "Minuto o (ticků)" (`missedByTicks`, how many ticks the price missed the limit order) in the Setup card, shown when "Stav naplnění" is not "naplněno" (NO_FILL / MISSED / SKIPPED), like in the "Setup bez vstupu" window. Until now an unfilled setup filled in as a trade ("Vyplnit jako obchod"), or a trade saved directly as no fill, had no way to enter or edit it. The form is prefilled from the setup, saved as a positive number, empty = not saved, and dropped when the state is switched to "naplněno". It also shows in the Setup card summary.

## 4.7.4 - 2026-10-07
- AI export (`ai-export/<id>.json`) now also carries the journal's dictionary customisation as `taxonomy` (custom options, renames, hidden, order – the same object as `settings.taxonomy`). Tools reading the export (Backtest Lab) can now recognise and label custom keys like `CUSTOM_FIX_RRR` instead of reporting them as unknown. Other settings stay out of the export. The file refreshes on the next save, or via "přegenerovat AI export".

## 4.7.3 - 2026-10-04
- New: an unfilled limit order or a consciously skipped setup can be filled in as a complete hypothetical trade ("what would it have done"). In the "Setup bez vstupu" window there is a new button "Vyplnit jako celý obchod" (saves the setup, then opens the full trade form), and every setup row in the Deník has "Vyplnit jako obchod". The form is prefilled from the setup (planned entry → entry price, SL, target 1, setup, entry levels, order flow, fill state, reason + note → comment, 1 contract, template commission) and the trade replaces the setup under the same id. "Minuto o" and "došlo na" stay on the trade; the setup's max favourable move is kept as `setupMaxFavorableTicks` (the trade's `maxFavorableTicks` is a computed field).
- A hypothetical trade is a normal trade whose "Stav naplnění" is not "naplněno": NO_FILL and MISSED form the "no fill" group, SKIPPED the "vědomě vynechané" group. No new data field.
- New: two switches next to "včetně obchodů, které bych naživo nevzal" (Přehled účtu, Deník, Kalendář, Reporty): "včetně no fill obchodů" and "včetně vědomě vynechaných". Same behaviour as the existing switch: off by default, remembered (localStorage FJ_includeNoFill / FJ_includeSkipped), hypothetical trades are always visible in the list, the switches decide only whether they count in P/L, equity, win rate, drawdown, risk limits, calendar, reports, account overview and instrument share. A trade that is both "naživo bych nevzal" and no fill needs both switches. Fill rate is unchanged and counts them by their state.
- Visible change: an existing trade saved with "Stav naplnění" = NO_FILL / MISSED / SKIPPED now drops out of P/L by default, until the matching switch is on.
- New: Deník filter "Stav naplnění" (all / only filled / only no fill / only consciously skipped), also applies to setups without entry.
- New: Přehled účtu blocks "No fill – hypoteticky" and "Vědomě vynechané – hypoteticky" (count, P/L, win rate, expectancy, expectancy without / with them, breakdown by state resp. setup), next to the "Naživo bych nevzal" block.
- Deník: hypothetical trade cards are tinted (blue = no fill, grey = skipped) with a "hypoteticky" badge. The day header shows "N× no fill" / "N× vědomě vynechán" and, when switches exclude trades of that day, their P/L as a muted "mimo: ±$X" next to the day P/L (a day with only hypothetical trades no longer shows a bare +$0.00).
- Trade form: for a not-filled state the "Pohyb ceny" card (MFE/MAE, after-exit ticks) applies as soon as the trade has an exit price; MFE/MAE derivation and the "Neúplné" badge follow the same rule. A hint under "Stav naplnění" says the trade is hypothetical.

## 4.7.2 - 2026-10-02
- New: a journal can be hidden (Nastavení → Obecné → "Deníky", buttons Skrýt / Zobrazit). A hidden journal is not shown or counted anywhere: journal selector, Přehled účtu (incl. "Všechny deníky"), Podíl instrumentů, Kalendář, Reporty, the move-trade target list and the dictionary copy target. Its data stays, goes into backups and into the AI export, and it can be shown again at any time. Stored as `hidden:true` on the journal profile (only when true). Logic in the new module app/journals.js (FJJournals).
- Hiding the active journal asks first and then switches to the last used visible journal (localStorage FJ_previousJournalId), otherwise the first visible one. The last visible journal cannot be hidden. If the active journal is hidden at startup (e.g. after restoring a backup), the same fallback applies; if every journal is hidden, the first one is shown again.
- New: Nastavení → Deníky → "Přehled účtu po spuštění ukáže": last active journal (default) or all journals (localStorage FJ_overviewStartJournal). Before, Přehled účtu always started with all journals. "Vymazat filtry" returns to this default.
- New: any journal (also a hidden or non-active one) can be deleted completely from Nastavení → Deníky; the 🗑️ in the sidebar uses the same flow. Confirmation requires typing the journal's exact name. Deleting removes the journal file, its AI-export copy and the screenshots no other journal uses (shared screenshots stay; if another journal cannot be read, no screenshot is deleted). safety-backups are kept. Account mappings pointing to the deleted journal are removed – before, the next NinjaTrader import would recreate the deleted journal file.
- Integrace → Přiřazení účtů: added a note and tooltip that "Skrýt" only hides the account from the list and does not stop the import – trades keep flowing to the mapped journal (unmapped → active journal). Hidden journals are not offered in the mapping, except the one an account is already mapped to, shown as "(skrytý)".
- Fixed: the calendar drew only the days of the shown month, so in a week crossing a month boundary the "Týden" total left out the other month's days (e.g. in October 2026 the week 28. 9. – 4. 10. missed the trades of 29. and 30. 9.). The grid now always runs in whole weeks Mon–Sun; days of the previous / next month are dimmed but show their P/L and can be clicked, and the week total covers all 7 days. The same week has the same total in both months.
- New: "Výsledek měsíce" card above the calendar grid: P/L of the calendar month, number of trades, win rate, profit factor and trading days (green ↑ / red ↓). It counts only the days of the shown month (not the neighbouring days in the grid) and follows the journal filter and the "naživo bych nevzal" switch.
- New: Nastavení → "Přizpůsobení" tab. It holds "Vzhled (téma)" (moved from Obecné, unchanged) and the new "Karty obchodu" card: for each card of the trade form – SL / TP, Pohyb ceny, Setup, Hladiny, Komentáře – "Zobrazit" and "Upozorňovat na nevyplněné". Default: everything shown and warned. One setting for the whole app (localStorage FJ_tradeCards), saved immediately.
- A hidden card disappears from the trade form and its content from the trade card in Deník (Setup → setup / trend / fill pills and "Plán", Hladiny → the "Hladiny" block, SL / TP → "plán R", Komentáře → the comment). The data stay on the trade, are kept when the trade is saved, and go into CSV and the AI export. A hidden card is not checked.
- The per-card warning replaces the global "Označování neúplných obchodů" switch: the "⚠ Neúplné" badge and "chybí N" in the card headers count only cards that are shown and warned. Who had the old switch off gets every warning off (taken over once from FJ_markIncompleteTrades). Komentáře have nothing to check.
- Changed: "Hladiny" is a separate card of the trade form (hladina vstupu, SR proti targetu / S/L, order flow, type of target level 1 and 2); Setup keeps setup, fill status, trend, plan followed and "naživo bych to vzal". Field IDs and stored data are unchanged. The missing type of target level 1 now counts under Hladiny, its price under SL / TP. Logic: FJTaxonomy.TRADE_CARDS / missingContextByCard / missingForCards (taxonomy.js).
- Tests: tests/journal-visibility.test.js (fallback of the active journal, overview start journal, mapping list, complete deletion incl. shared screenshots), tests/calendar-weeks.test.js (whole weeks, week total across months, month result) and tests/trade-cards.test.js (cards, defaults, takeover of the old switch, badge, trade card, form); all mutation-checked; 296 tests. UI checked in Electron with a temporary data folder (not live data).

## 4.7.1 - 2026-09-30

Like MFE/MAE it is shown read-only ("7746 — dopočítáno z výstupní ceny") with an "upravit ručně" link; after an override the field offers "použít dopočet z výstupní ceny (…)". Target / BE trades keep an empty editable field. Before, the price was derived only on save and the form field stayed empty, so it had to be copied by hand.
- Fixed: re-saving a trade whose SL price was derived turned it into a manual value (slDerived=false), because the form loaded the derived value into the field as if it were typed. The field now remembers that the value is derived.

Checked: setup, fill status, trend, entry level, SR against target and against S/L, order flow confirmation, target level 1 (type and price), SL price, MFE, MAE, continuation and counter-move after exit. Derived values and zero count as filled; for an explicitly unfilled setup (NO_FILL, MISSED, SKIPPED…) the course / after-exit fields are not checked. Not checked: observed minutes, target level 2, "counter-move came first". The logic is FJTaxonomy.missingContextKeys (taxonomy.js).
- The badge replaces the old "neúplný kontext" pill (only MFE/MAE, only in journals flagged as backtest). The "Backtestový deník" flag is still stored but no longer drives any label.
- New: Nastavení → Obecné → "Označování neúplných obchodů" turns the badge on/off. One switch for the whole app (all journals), saved immediately, default on (localStorage FJ_markIncompleteTrades).
- New: "SR proti targetu" and "SR proti S/L" have a checkbox "žádná hladina v cestě" (srTargetNone / srStopLossNone, stored only as true and only without rows), so "there was nothing" differs from "not filled in". It cannot be ticked while rows exist (rows are never deleted by it); ticked, it disables "＋ Přidat hladinu". The card and the collapsed "Hladiny" header show "SR→TG: žádná". When merging targets it survives only if every leg has it and no leg has a level. The AI export carries it as stored.
- Changed: the cards in Nastavení are stacked in one column and each can be collapsed / expanded (click the heading). Which cards are open is remembered on this computer; by default all are collapsed. The tabs (Obecné, Riziko, Šablony, Číselníky, Data a zálohy) stay.
- Fixed: the unsaved-changes check in the trade form ignored checkboxes (it read "on" regardless of the tick).
- New: "Cena targetu — pro výpočet R" next to the SL price. It is the price of target level 1 (one field, not a second one); for a trade that ended on target it is filled in from the exit price, read-only with "upravit ručně", like the SL price; for a stopped-out trade it is typed in. With several targets TP1 → target 1 and TP2 → target 2 ("Cena targetu 2" appears only for a trade with another target). Two targets closed at the same price are one target. Derived prices carry targetLevel1PriceDerived / targetLevel2PriceDerived; merging targets takes only the type from a leg's derived price and the form derives it again from the legs.

- Changed: the trade form is split into collapsible cards under the always-visible basics (prices, P/L, contracts, extra targets): "SL / TP", "Pohyb ceny" (MFE, MAE, ticks after exit, observed minutes), "Setup" (setup, fill status, trend, entry level, SR, order flow, target level types, plan followed, would take it live) and "Komentáře" (texts and screenshots). ## 4.7.0 - 2026-09-29
- New: a trade can be marked "Naživo bych to vzal? → Ne – vzato jen kvůli měření" (wouldSkipLive) with a reason from a new editable dictionary "Důvod „naživo bych nevzal“" (LIQUIDITY_SWEPT, SR_IN_WAY, CONTEXT, TOO_LATE, LOW_CONVICTION, RISK_RULE, OTHER; immutable key, editable label, hiding, custom options, like the other dictionaries). It is a separate question from "Dodržen obchodní plán?" and the two are never merged.
- Such trades are by default excluded from equity, P/L, win rate, expectancy, drawdown, risk-management limits, the calendar, reports, the account overview, the instrument share and the day P/L in the Deník. They still count in the fill rate (whether a limit order filled is entry mechanics, not a decision). All of it goes through one filter, performanceRecords(), which every performance aggregation applies on its own.
- A checkbox "včetně obchodů, které bych naživo nevzal" in Přehled účtu, Deník, Kalendář and Reporty puts them back into all numbers so the two groups can be compared. It is one switch for the whole app and is remembered after a restart.
- The group is also tracked separately: a new block "Naživo bych nevzal" in Přehled účtu shows its count, P/L, win rate, expectancy, a breakdown by reason and "Expectancy bez nich / s nimi", regardless of the switch – to see whether skipping those setups costs the strategy anything.
- In the Deník such a trade has an orange-tinted card with a dashed border and a badge (styled like "⚡ Auto import", different colour) showing the reason's label. The day header says "N× naživo bych nevzal". New list filter: all / only these / without these.
- Changed: "SR proti targetu" and "SR proti S/L" are now lists of rows { level, price, ticksFromEntry } instead of a plain multi-select. One level = one row; the same type can appear more than once. Entering the price computes the distance from entry and vice versa (ticksFromEntry = |price − entry| / tick; price = entry ± ticks × tick). The distance is always positive; the side comes from the field (SR→TG in the trade's direction, SR→SL against it) and the trade's side. Changing entry price, side or instrument recomputes the other value. The tick size comes from the instrument template (or the built-in table for ES/MES/NQ/MNQ/YM/MYM/RTY/M2K, as for MFE/MAE); when the tick size or entry price is missing (or the side, for price from ticks) the link is switched off and both fields are saved as typed – nothing is ever estimated. Opening a trade recomputes nothing. A price on the wrong side of the entry shows a soft warning.
- Existing trades: srTarget / srStopLoss saved as keys are converted to rows without price or distance on first start (backup "pred-prevodem-sr-hladin" in safety-backups first; only the shape changes, custom keys are kept). Reading accepts the old shape everywhere, so journals that have not been opened yet keep working.
- Merging targets (combineTradeObjects): the SR rows of all legs are united (identical rows once; a bare row without price and distance gives way to the same level with values), and wouldSkipLive / wouldSkipReason survive the merge.
- Both CSV exports: the SR columns are now blocks "SR→TG n hladina / cena / ticky" and "SR→SL n …", one block per row (as many as the export needs, at least one), plus "Naživo bych nevzal" and "Důvod (naživo)". The AI export carries the rows as objects and the new fields as they are stored.
- Fixed: the tick / MFE / MAE migration (runTickFieldsMigration, 4.6.5) ran and rewrote the whole journal on every start, even when there was nothing to fix. Its marker tickFieldsVersion was not in the whitelist of top-level keys that main.js keeps when reading a journal (readJournal), so it was dropped on every read. It is now kept, and the migration runs once per journal as intended.
- Tests: tests/would-skip-live.test.js and tests/sr-levels.test.js (mutation-checked: removing the filter in performanceRecords or in dayKpis makes them fail); a new test in tests/journal-write.test.js checks that tickFieldsVersion survives a read / write round-trip in the main process (fails without the fix); 229 tests.

## 4.6.7 - 2026-09-26
- Fixed: opening and saving a trade with several targets could silently change its MFE / MAE. The form only has the exit price and result of the FIRST target, and it recomputed MFE / MAE from them: a value derived from the merged legs (e.g. 22 t from TP2) was replaced by 10 t from TP1 and saved as a MANUAL value, and with BE as the first target it was deleted. The form now passes all targets to the calculations (postExitDraftFromForm builds the legs the same way saving does), does not re-derive a multi-target trade, shows the value derived from the merge as read-only "dopočítáno ze sloučených cílů" and saves it with the Derived flag. Adding or removing a target in the form redraws the calculations.
- Fixed: a merged imported trade was calculated with the tick size of the ACTIVE journal (or with no settings at all before login) instead of the target journal, so merging a leg wiped exitTicks / maxFavorableTicks / maxAdverseTicks that the single-leg import had just computed. combineTradeObjects now takes the target journal's settings.
- Changed: import no longer derives MFE / MAE from the exit (introduced in 4.6.6 as a side effect). An imported trade's result is only the sign of its P/L and it has no stop-loss or target price, so every manual losing exit got an "MAE" equal to its exit loss and every winning one an "MFE" equal to its exit profit, without anyone seeing it. Imported trades now carry only measured values (NinjaTrader) plus the calculated fields; the form still shows the derivation as a read-only value.
- Fixed: MFE / MAE from a non-NinjaTrader event were labelled "naměřeno v NinjaTraderu". The source is now stored as it came (nt8 for NinjaTrader, otherwise e.g. tradingview) and the form shows "naměřeno konektorem (…)". Any measured value ranks above a derived one when legs merge.
- Changed: a value measured by a connector no longer offers "použít dopočet z výstupní ceny" – the measurement ranks above arithmetic and one click would have replaced it with a worse number. Once the user edits the value it is manual and the link appears as usual.
- Fixed: NT8 connector could send NaN / Infinity as a price extreme, which made the JSON invalid; the app rejected the whole exit execution and the trade never reached the journal. The connector now sends only finite prices (IsUsablePrice), and main.js also rejects the NT sentinel, overflowing values and an entry price of 0 (position from a snapshot without a price). Connector version raised to 3, so users with version 2 get the one-time re-install notice.
- Fixed: the one-time connector notice (shown 800 ms after login) could overwrite a confirmation that was already open (e.g. deleting a trade); that confirmation's answer was lost. Confirmations now queue, and the notice waits until no window is open.
- Fixed: when the NinjaTrader 8 folder is not found, and after "Uložit konektor", the app told the user to import the .cs file via Tools → Import → NinjaScript Add-On, which accepts only an exported .zip archive. It now says to copy the file into NinjaTrader 8\bin\Custom\AddOns and press F5 in the NinjaScript Editor.
- Fixed: the connector status looked only at AddOns\FuturesJournalCapture.cs, so an outdated copy under another name never triggered the notice. It now counts every copy of the class in Custom and the oldest version decides.
- Changed: the search for connector copies in Custom runs asynchronously and skips NinjaTrader's built-in scripts (@…), so it no longer blocks the main process (and incoming executions) while reading hundreds of files. "Uložit konektor" and the automatic installation share one template helper.
- Fixed: the 4.6.6 entry below had a broken path (missing backslashes and a stray control character).
- Fixed: VYTVORIT_INSTALATOR.bat seemed stuck at "[2/3]" for tens of minutes when run from a freshly extracted release folder. Every new folder started without node_modules, so npm wrote about 7,500 files onto the (external USB) drive, and Windows Defender scanned each of them. That ran at about 10 files/s while npm printed nothing. The project itself did not change: package-lock.json is identical since 4.6.2. The installer is now built in one persistent workspace on the internal disk (%LOCALAPPDATA%\FuturesJournalPRO-build). Sources are mirrored there with robocopy, the build components install only the first time (or when they change), and the finished Setup exe is copied back into dist\ next to the bat, so double-clicking works as before. Each step prints its start time, the end prints the total time, and a lock stops two builds from sharing the workspace; a lock left by a closed window can be taken over with A.
- The NinjaTrader add-on has to be re-installed and recompiled (F5 in the NinjaScript Editor) to get the NaN fix.

## 4.6.6 - 2026-09-26
- MFE on a trade that ended on target and MAE on a trade that ended on the stop loss are no longer pre-filled into an editable field. They follow from the exit price by definition, and a filled-in field looked like a measurement. The form now shows the value as grey read-only text ("dopočítáno z výstupní ceny") in place of the field, with an "upravit ručně" link. Target: MFE read-only, MAE editable. Stop loss: MAE read-only, MFE editable. Breakeven / manual: both editable. Each trade therefore asks for exactly one of the two numbers.
- "upravit ručně" moves the computed value into the field as a starting point, and from then on it behaves as an ordinary manual value (for a manually closed trade the equality does not hold). A manual value that differs from the computed one offers "použít dopočet z výstupní ceny (N t)" to go back. A field emptied while typing stays a field instead of snapping back to read-only.
- MFE / MAE now come from NinjaTrader automatically. NinjaTrader keeps the highest and lowest price reached on every entry execution of an account while the position is open (Execution.MaxPrice / MinPrice). This was checked in NinjaTrader.sqlite: the values are there for ATM trades and for plain manual trades without any strategy. They are missing only where NT had no market data during the trade. The connector sends them with every exit execution (excursionMaxPrice / excursionMinPrice + tickSize). main.js turns them into ticks from the position's average entry price, including the exit price itself, and the trade stores them as mfeTicks / maeTicks with mfeTicksSource / maeTicksSource = 'nt8'. Missing extremes send nothing; they are never replaced by zero.
- Precedence is manual > NinjaTrader > computed from the exit. The derived pre-fill never overwrites an NT8 value. When legs merge, the maximum is taken only within the highest tier present, so a later imported leg cannot override a value typed by hand. Typing over an NT8 value in the form turns it into a manual value. The field shows "naměřeno v NinjaTraderu" while it is untouched.
- An imported trade with a single exit now gets its calculated fields (exitTicks, maxFavorableTicks, maxAdverseTicks, …) right at import. Until now it got them only after being opened and saved in the form, so the NinjaTrader MFE/MAE did not reach them. Only merged trades were calculated at import. The tick size comes from the target journal's instrument templates, the same as the commission. Found while verifying on Market Replay.
- One-time notice for users who have an older NinjaTrader 8 connector installed: "Pro pokročilé funkce a stabilitu doporučujeme přeinstalovat NT8 konektor…", with "Přeinstalovat teď" and "Později". It appears after login, only when the connector in NinjaTrader is older than the one the app carries. The connector now has a version marker (FJ_CONNECTOR_VERSION); a connector without it counts as version 1. It appears once per connector version, whatever the user chooses. New users and users who already re-installed never see it.
- The automatic connector installation now writes exactly one file in one place (Documents\NinjaTrader 8\bin\Custom\AddOns\FuturesJournalCapture.cs) and nothing else. Until now it created the whole folder path even when NinjaTrader was not there (for example Documents redirected to OneDrive). It now writes nothing in that case and points to the manual export + import in NinjaTrader. Only a missing AddOns folder inside an existing Custom folder is created. No backup copy of the old file is made, because NinjaTrader would compile it as a second FuturesJournalCapture class. If another file in Custom already declares that class, the installation stops and names the file, since NinjaTrader would not compile either.
- The NinjaTrader add-on has to be re-installed and recompiled (F5 in the NinjaScript Editor) for the new fields to be sent. Trades imported before that keep what they had.

## 4.6.5 - 2026-09-26
- New record type: "Setup bez vstupu" (recordType SETUP_ONLY, default TRADE), button "＋ Setup bez vstupu" in the Deník. Light form: date, time, instrument, side, setup, entry levels, order flow, planned entry price, SL, target level 1, status (NO_FILL | SKIPPED), missed by ticks, reason, note, optionally max favorable ticks and the furthest level reached (maxLevel, a "Typ cílové hladiny" key). No exit price, P/L, commission, points or R. Shown in the trade list as a dashed tinted row with a ◌ icon.
- Setup-only records are excluded from all eight aggregations (computeDrawdownByDate, evaluateDayRisk, computeOverallStats, renderDashboard, renderTrades, renderCalendar, filteredOverviewTrades, generateReportView) and from getCalendarRows, so they never reach equity, P/L, drawdown or the risk-management counters. The in-memory `trades` list holds only trades; setups live in `setupRecords`, and every aggregation filters again with tradeRecords() on its own. tests/setup-only.test.js feeds each of the eight a mix of trades and deliberately dangerous setups and fails if any output changes.
- They do enter setup frequency and fill rate (new "Setupy a fill rate" block in the dashboard): fill rate = FILLED / (FILLED + NO_FILL); a trade without a stored fill status counts as filled; SKIPPED (and MISSED) are not in the rate and are reported separately ("Vynechané", "Nestihnuto"). The JSON backup now includes setup records. The pill "neúplný kontext" is never shown for them, and the schema migrations skip them.
- New trade-course fields next to the existing post-exit ones: mfeTicks (furthest in the trade's direction until exit, from the entry price) and maeTicks (furthest against it), both in ticks and always positive, plus observedMinutes (how long the price was watched after exit, optional). postExitFavorableTicks / postExitAdverseTicks are unchanged and still measured from the exit price. The 15-minute rule is gone; there is no fixed window.
- New stored fields: exitTicks (signed exit vs entry), maxFavorableTicks = max(mfe, exitTicks + postExitFavorable), maxAdverseTicks = max(mae, postExitAdverse - exitTicks), touchedSl = maxAdverseTicks >= slTicks. When an input is missing the result is computed from what is there; when all are missing it is empty, never 0. Without maeTicks a negative post-exit result is "unknown", not a measured zero, so it stays empty. Stopped trades keep their values (at least the SL distance; touchedEntry/touchedSl stay empty for them, as before).
- One-off migration per journal (backup first): maxAdverseTicks = 0 on winning trades without MAE (an artefact, not a measurement) is cleared, and exitTicks / maxFavorableTicks are added to existing trades.
- New editable dictionary "Typ cílové hladiny" (LIQUIDITY, VPOC_DAY, VPOC_30M, VPOC_1M, VAH, VAL, VWAP, LVN, GAP_EDGE, LTA_LEVEL, TRAIL_M2, MANUAL_EXIT): immutable key, editable label, hiding, custom options, like the entry levels. Trades carry targetLevel1 / targetLevel2 (type + price), entered in the "Hladiny" section.
- Soft warnings only: SL further than 40 ticks ("SL nepravděpodobně daleko, překlep?") in the form and on the card; a filled trade without MFE or MAE shows "neúplný kontext" on the card.
- Both CSV exports, the AI export and the merge whitelist carry the new fields. A merged trade takes MFE/MAE as the maximum over its legs.
- MFE/MAE are pre-filled where the trade itself implies them: result target -> mfeTicks = exitTicks, result stoploss -> maeTicks = |exitTicks|, breakeven / manual -> nothing. Not filled when the exit price differs from the target level price (targetLevel1/2) or from the SL price, and not for multi-leg trades. The value carries mfeTicksDerived / maeTicksDerived (same pattern as slDerived), is shown in italics with the tooltip "odvozeno z výstupní ceny", and typing over it clears the flag. A manual value is never overwritten by recalculation, merge or import; a derived one is refreshed when the exit changes and dropped when it no longer matches.
- "Neúplný kontext" is now shown only in journals marked as backtest (new checkbox "Backtestový deník" in the journal name dialog, stored as backtest:true on the journal profile) and never for NO_FILL / MISSED / SKIPPED. The CSV column of the same name still states the raw fact.

## 4.6.4 - 2026-09-25
- The levels collapse in the Deník list too. A trade card used to spell out its entry level, both SR fields and the order flow confirmations as five or six rows of tags, so a day with three trades barely fit on screen. They now sit behind a "Hladiny (N)" item on the card, the same four groups that collapse in the form. Setup, Trend and the fill status stay visible, so what the trade was is still readable at a glance.
- Expanding one trade's levels survives the list being redrawn — saving another trade or changing a filter no longer closes it — because the expanded trades are remembered by id rather than in the page.
- Each day header got a button that expands or collapses the levels of every trade in that day at once, and it says which of the two it will do. It appears only on days that have something to expand, and it no longer closes the day itself while doing it.
- The "Hladiny" header is now blue, bold and carries its own arrow, in the form and on the card. As plain grey text at label size it was indistinguishable from the labels around it, and there was nothing to suggest it could be clicked at all.
- The trade form is shorter. Six of the "Obchodní plán k tomuto obchodu" fields are gone from it — instrument, maximum trades, loss limit, daily target and the two times stayed empty and only pushed the screenshots off the bottom of the window. Setup / scénář, Psychologický checklist and the note, now called Komentář k setupu, stay: they are filled in on an ordinary trade. Values saved in the six removed fields are kept on the trades that have them — the trade card still shows them, both CSV exports still carry them, and editing a trade no longer wipes them.
- The level fields (Hladina vstupu, SR proti targetu, SR proti S/L) and Order flow potvrzení now sit inside one collapsible "Hladiny" item, closed every time the form opens. Its header lists what is selected, so collapsing hides nothing — an edited trade shows its levels without being expanded first. Trend stays outside it: three tiles are not worth collapsing.
- The app no longer writes into the comment field. Merging targets of one position used to leave "Sloučeno z N cílů stejné pozice (TP1/TP2/BE)." there — even on a single-target automatic import, so the journal filled up with a sentence nobody wrote and that said nothing about the trade. The same went for "Ručně zadáno s N cíli" on a multi-target trade entered by hand. Comments written by hand on individual targets are still joined together on a merge.
- A trade can now record what the price did AFTER the exit: two numbers in ticks, measured from the exit price and always entered as positive values — how far the price carried on in the trade's direction, and how far it went against it. A third field marks that the move against came first. The direction is carried by the field, not by a sign, so a long and a short that behaved identically are entered identically. The fields sit right below the stop loss price in the trade form. They are hidden, and nothing is stored, for a trade whose fill status says the setup was NOT filled (limit not filled, missed, deliberately skipped) — there is nothing to measure after an exit that never happened. A blank fill status counts as filled, because a manually entered trade normally leaves it blank and hiding the fields there would mean never getting to them.
- A hint under the fields says which number goes where: the continuation is measured in the trade's own direction, the move against it in the opposite one, both from the exit price and both as positive numbers. On a trade that ended on the stop loss, the continuation figure answers a different question — how much wider the stop would have had to be to survive — and the hint says so.
- From those two numbers the journal works out and stores five more: the risk in ticks (entry to stop loss), the furthest the price got in the trade's direction measured from the entry, the furthest it got against the position measured from the entry, and whether the move after the exit came back as far as the entry price or all the way to the stop loss. They are stored on the trade, not merely displayed, because they are the input for the planned analyses (stop loss sweep, the SL × TP grid) and recomputing them later would need the tick size of a contract that may no longer be in the templates.
- On a trade that ended on the stop loss, the two "did the price come back" flags are deliberately left empty. The exit price on such a trade IS the stop loss price, so by definition both flags would read "yes" no matter what the price did afterwards; used as a filter they would only separate stopped trades from the rest. How far the price actually went against the position is what "max against" says, and that is the figure to analyse there.
- A field that has no input stays EMPTY and is never filled in with a zero. With no stop loss price there is no risk in ticks and no "max against"; with no trade direction there is no "in the trade's direction"; with no tick size there are no tick figures at all. Zero would mean "the price did not move" and in the analysis it could not be told apart from "not filled in".
- Validation is soft and never blocks saving. A negative number is flagged at the field ("enter it as a positive number, the field carries the direction") and stored as its absolute value; text that is not a number is flagged as well. The whole form stays optional, as before.
- Instrument templates gained a "Tick" column — the tick size, the smallest price move. ES/MES/NQ/MNQ (0.25), YM/MYM (1) and RTY/M2K (0.1) are built in, as with the point value. For any other contract it is NOT guessed: until the tick size is filled into the template, the tick figures stay empty and the form says why.
- Both CSV exports (Deník and the cross-journal Reporty) carry all eight new columns, the entered ones and the calculated ones, and the AI export mirror carries them too. The header row and the data row of each export now come from a single function, so a column added to one of them cannot silently shift every following value by one cell.
- Merging several targets of one position keeps the new fields. They are taken from the leg with the LATEST exit time, because what matters is how the price behaved after the position actually ended, not after a partial exit in the middle; if that leg is empty, the next one backwards is used so a filled-in value is never dropped. The calculated fields are worked out again from the merged trade, and the measurement starts from the last leg's exit price rather than the volume-weighted average.

## 4.6.3 - 2026-09-25
- Fixed the crash behind "Aplikace se neočekávaně ukončila. Podrobnosti byly uloženy do chybového protokolu.", which mostly hit while saving a closed trade. Screenshots were stored as base64 inside the journal's own JSON file, so the journal had grown to 138 MB for 120 trades — of which 0.1 MB was actual trade data and the rest images. Every single save shipped that whole file from the renderer to the main process, serialized it, wrote it, and then re-rendered every thumbnail twice (once as the image source and once inside its click handler). The renderer ran out of memory and Electron killed it; the error log recorded it as "Renderer process ended: {"reason":"crashed"}".
- Screenshots now live as ordinary files in journal-data/images and the journal keeps only a short reference to each one. The file name is a hash of the image's contents, so the same screenshot is never stored twice and moving a trade between journals copies nothing. The app reads them through its own fjimg:// address, so a thumbnail is loaded from disk only when it is actually shown, and never travels through inter-process messages.
- Existing journals are converted automatically the first time they are opened after the update. The conversion writes the image files first, makes a safety copy of the journal, and only then rewrites it — an interruption at any point leaves the original untouched and the conversion simply runs again next time. On the 138 MB journal it took under a second and left a 0.19 MB file; every image was verified to come back byte for byte identical.
- New screenshots are scaled to at most 2560 px on the longer edge and re-encoded as WebP before they are stored, which typically cuts a chart screenshot to a fraction of its size. If the conversion would make the file bigger or fails for any reason, the original is kept — an image is never degraded or dropped to save space.
- Full backups are now assembled and restored by the main process instead of being built in the renderer and passed across as one giant string. Screenshots are written into the backup file as base64 as before, so a backup stays self-contained and can be restored on a machine that has no image folder.
- Backups now include the daily notes (comment, preview, notes and their images). They were missing from the backup file, so restoring one silently wiped every day note.
- Fixed screenshots disappearing from a trade that was never saved. Opening a trade for editing handed the form the trade's own image list rather than a copy of it, so removing a screenshot took effect immediately — closing the window without saving did not bring it back, and the loss became permanent the next time anything wrote the journal. Saving a trade already made the copy on the way out; it was only missing on the way in.
- The same shallow copy was missing on day notes: reading one shared its image list with the stored note, so adding or removing a screenshot changed the stored data before the save had actually happened. A day note now changes only when it is saved.
- The journal in memory now changes only after the write to disk has actually succeeded. Until now every save changed the data in memory first and wrote afterwards, so a failed write — a full disk, a locked file, anything — left the app showing a change that existed nowhere, announced only by a single message. That change then either quietly stuck or quietly vanished the next time anything else saved successfully, depending on which write came first. Saving a trade, deleting one, merging trades, day notes, settings, the enumerations and both bulk operations (marking legacy points, filling in trade direction) all go through one write path now; if it fails, the app keeps showing exactly what the journal really holds.
- Both bulk operations also work out every change before writing anything, instead of editing trades one by one as they go, so a failed write can no longer leave half of them applied. They now match trades by id rather than by position in the list, which an auto-import arriving mid-operation used to be able to shift.

## 4.6.2 - 2026-09-23
- Fixed a crash that made the journal impossible to open at all: the enumeration editor referenced a variable before it was declared, and since it renders while the journal loads, the error surfaced as "Databázi se nepodařilo otevřít: Cannot access sourceLabel before initialization". The settings rendering had no test coverage, which is how it shipped; it now has smoke tests that render the editor, the transfer panel and the chip grids against a stub DOM, and they reproduce this exact failure when the fix is reverted.

- There is no delete, by design. Deleting an option would leave the trades that use it holding a value outside the enumeration. Hiding is the replacement: a hidden option still counts in statistics, still shows on the trades that have it, and is still offered when editing such a trade so its value cannot be silently dropped. Copying and importing both name what they are about to overwrite and ask first.

Neither copying nor importing touches trades — a trade stores keys, so only labels and which options are offered can change.

- The two SR fields share their option list with Hladina vstupu, because a level is a level whether it is where you entered or what stands in the way. A level renamed or added once therefore applies to all three fields; hiding and ordering can still be set per field. All three new fields are editable in Nastavení → Číselníky like the existing ones, and they travel with the copy/export/import above.

## 4.5.1 - 2026-09-08

   ASAR je způsob, jakým Electron balí zdrojové soubory appky do jednoho

     "extraResources") – přesně tak, jak to Electron pro podobné případy
     doporučuje.

     zpátky do vlastní instalační složky appky (do ASAR archivu se totiž
     nedá zapisovat, jen číst).
   - "asar" nastaveno na true.

   Funkčnost pro tebe: naprosto beze změny – jen appka teď startuje o

Futures Journal PRO 4.4.2 – změny oproti 4.4.1

1) POKUS O ODSTRANĚNÍ NPM VAROVÁNÍ boolean@3.2.0 – ZALOŽENO NA SKUTEČNÉM ZDROJI
   Díky odkazu, který jsi našel (github.com/electron/get/issues/304,
   navazuje na github.com/electron/electron/issues/44416), se podařilo
   dohledat PŘESNÝ řetězec závislosti přímo z hlášení Electron týmu:

     electron -> @electron/get@2.0.3 -> global-agent@3.0.0 -> boolean@3.2.0
                                          (i přes roarr@2.15.4 -> boolean, stejná verze)

   Zjištění: balíček "boolean" byl svým autorem označen jako CELÝ
   nepodporovaný (potvrzeno přímo na jeho stránce na npmjs.com – "This
   package has been deprecated", i jeho aktuální/poslední verze 3.2.0 nese
   tohle označení). To znamená, že předchozí pokus v 4.0.0/4.1.0-4.1.4
   (override "boolean": "latest") nemohl fungovat – "latest" u tohoto
   balíčku JE ta samá deprecated verze 3.2.0, žádná novější "opravená"
   verze neexistuje.

   Skutečná páka: "global-agent" (balíček, který "boolean" vyžaduje) má
   mezitím vlastní novou hlavní verzi 4.1.3 (aktuálně nejnovější), zatímco
   @electron/get má napevno danou starou 3.0.0. Balíček "boolean" je
   triviální pomocník (převod textu na true/false) – přesně to, co se při
   přechodu na novou hlavní verzi typicky jako první zbaví. V package.json
   je proto nově override "global-agent": "^4.1.3" (nahrazuje předchozí,
   neúčinný override "boolean").

   POCTIVĚ K JISTOTĚ TOHOTO ŘEŠENÍ: nepodařilo se mi přímo ověřit soubor
   package.json balíčku global-agent@4.1.3 (nedostupné z mého prostředí),
   takže nemám 100% jistotu, že novější global-agent na "boolean" už
   opravdu nezávisí – je to podložený odhad (velký skok hlavní verze +
   triviální povaha "boolean"), ne potvrzený fakt. Navíc jde o citlivější
   zásah než u glob/rimraf: global-agent se v @electron/get používá při
   STAHOVÁNÍ SAMOTNÉHO ELECTRON BINÁRNÍHO SOUBORU během "npm install" (řeší
   nastavení proxy), takže existuje malé riziko, že by vynucená novější
   verze mohla tenhle konkrétní krok rozbít.

   CO SLEDOVAT: pokud po "npm install" varování o "boolean" zmizí a
   instalace proběhne v pořádku až do konce (včetně "npm run dist") –
   skvělé. Pokud by "npm install" selhalo konkrétně na stažení Electronu
   (ne jen na varování), dej vědět – override "global-agent" se dá vrátit
   zpět bez dopadu na cokoliv jiného.

Futures Journal PRO 4.3.8 – změny oproti 4.3.7

1) OPRAVA: KOMISE U STOP LOSS OBCHODU ŠTRÁTU CHYBNĚ SNIŽOVALA
   Automatický výpočet (zavedený v 4.3.3/4.3.4) vždy počítal "hrubý zisk
   mínus komise" bez ohledu na to, jestli šlo o Target, nebo Stop Loss.
   U zisku je to správně (komise zisk snižuje), ale u ztráty je to obráceně
   – komise je náklad navíc, takže ztrátu MÁ zvětšovat, ne zmenšovat.
   Potvrzeno na reálném příkladu: obchod -1,75 bodu, 1 kontrakt MES (hrubá
   ztráta $8,75), komise $3 – aplikace dřív spočítala čistou ztrátu jako
   $5,75 (chybně nižší), teď správně $11,75 (hrubá ztráta + komise).

   Zisk (Target): čistý = hrubý − komise (beze změny, bylo správně).
   Ztráta (Stop Loss): čistý = hrubý + komise (opraveno).
   Break Even: čistý = −komise (obchod bez pohybu ceny pořád stojí komisi).

   Platí pro první cíl (TP1) i pro každý další ručně přidaný cíl (TP2, Break
   Even...) v témže formuláři.

2) Přepnutí pole "Výsledek" (Target/Stop Loss/Break Even) teď rovnou přepočítá
   zisk/ztrátu podle výše uvedené opravené logiky – dřív bylo potřeba po
   přepnutí ještě sáhnout na jiné pole, aby se přepočet spustil.

    Předchozí verze používala prohlížečové dialogy window.prompt(). V náhledu
    spouštěném v sandboxovaném prostředí (a možná i jinde) se ukázalo, že
    window.prompt() může tiše vrátit prázdnou hodnotu bez zobrazení dialogu,
    takže tlačítko vypadalo jako nereagující. "Změnit heslo" teď používá
    normální okno v rámci aplikace (stejné jako u ostatních akcí, např.

13) SKUTEČNÁ PŘÍČINA OPAKOVANÉHO "TP3 NAVÍC" U NINJATRADERU (MES) – 3 nezávislé opravy
    Minulá oprava (verze výše) přidala ochranu proti souběžnému zpracování a
    kontrolu duplicit podle ceny/času, ale problém se objevil znovu. Po bližším
    zkoumání se ukázaly TŘI samostatné mezery, opraveny jsou všechny tři:

    a) NinjaScript konektor (FuturesJournalCapture.cs): NinjaTrader umí znovu
       vyvolat událost ExecutionUpdate pro fill, který už jednou proběhl –
       typicky po výpadku a obnovení spojení s brokerem/simulátorem. Konektor
       teď v paměti (po dobu běhu NinjaTraderu) pamatuje, které ExecutionId už
       jednou odeslal, a opakované odeslání stejné exekuce přeskočí úplně u
       zdroje – TOHLE je pravděpodobně skutečná příčina.
    b) main.js: okno pro rozpoznání duplicitního hlášení (podle účtu,
       instrumentu, ceny, počtu kontraktů a času) bylo jen 5 sekund – pokud
       NinjaTrader exekuci nahlásí znovu s větším odstupem (např. při
       pomalejším obnovení spojení), okno to nestihlo zachytit. Prodlouženo
       na 20 minut.
    c) Skutečná díra (nejzávažnější): když se dva cíle (např. TP1 a TP2)
       sloučily do jednoho obchodu, sloučený obchod si u sebe pamatoval ID
       zdrojové události jen z PRVNÍHO cíle – ID druhého (a dalšího) cíle se
       při slučování ztratilo. Pokud pak NinjaTrader nahlásil TEN DRUHÝ fill
       znovu, aplikace neměla podle čeho poznat, že ho už jednou zpracovala,
       a přidala ho jako nový (třetí) cíl. Opraveno – každý cíl uvnitř
       sloučeného obchodu si teď své zdrojové ID pamatuje samostatně a kontrola
       duplicit je prochází všechny, ne jen ten první.

    Pro NOVOU verzi konektoru je potřeba v NinjaTraderu udělat znovu Compile
    (Tools → Import → NinjaScript Add-On, nebo přes automatickou instalaci v
    kartě Integrace) – bez toho se oprava (a) neprojeví.

14) OPRAVA: OBČAS "ZASEKNUTÉ" POLE PO PŘEPNUTÍ DENÍKU (nereaguje na klik ani psaní)
    Pravděpodobná příčina: known Electron/Chromium chování, kdy obnovení
    stránky (location.reload()) v okamžiku, kdy má nějaké pole fokus, může
    po znovunačtení nechat některé pole (nemusí to být zrovna to původní)
    "zaseknuté" – reaguje, až když uživatel klikne jinam a zpátky.
    Všech 6 míst v aplikaci, která dělají reload (přepnutí deníku, nový/
    přejmenovaný/smazaný deník, obnova zálohy, smazání dat, kompletní reset),
    teď před reloadem nejdřív úmyslně zruší fokus z aktuálního pole.
    Pro jistotu přidána i druhá pojistka: zavřené modální okno (např. staré
    okno pro sloučení obchodů) teď má nastaveno pointer-events:none, takže
    ani v případě chyby ve viditelnosti nemůže neviditelně blokovat kliky na
    prvky pod sebou.

15) SKUTEČNÁ PŘÍČINA "ZASEKNUTÝCH" POLÍ – NALEZENO (jen v nainstalované aplikaci)
    Bod 14) výše (zrušení fokusu před reloadem) byl užitečná pojistka, ale
    nebyl to skutečný zdroj problému – proto přetrvával i po něm, zatímco
    v náhledu v prohlížeči (kde tahle věc vůbec nehrozí) fungovalo vše v
    pořádku. Skutečná příčina: aplikace se na Windows spouští automaticky na
    pozadí (skrytě) po přihlášení, a když se pak okno poprvé zobrazí – buď
    hned po instalaci/spuštění, nebo kliknutím na ikonu u hodin – volalo se
    jen okno.show(), bez okno.focus(). Okno se vizuálně zobrazilo a myš
    fungovala normálně (proto šlo měnit číslo šipkami u pole), ale klávesnice
    zůstávala "mimo" okno, dokud uživatel neklikl jinam a zpět – to je known
    chování Electronu na Windows.
    Opraveno: úplně všechna místa, která okno zobrazují (první spuštění,
    kliknutí na ikonu u hodin, druhé spuštění aplikace, obnovení z
    minimalizace), teď volají i .focus(), a na Windows navíc krátké
    přepnutí "vždy nahoře", což OS donutí klávesnicový fokus oknu opravdu
    předat – tohle druhé opatření řeší i případy, kdy samotné .focus()
    nestačí (to je bohužel taky known chování Electronu na Windows).
